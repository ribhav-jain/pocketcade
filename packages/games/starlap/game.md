faq:
  - q: What is a Perfect?
    a: Pip's centre lands on the gold centre of the star. A Perfect scores 1 point plus 1 for each Perfect in a row, up to 5 points each. A Good, a little off the centre, scores 1 and breaks the row.
  - q: What changes as I go?
    a: Pip speeds up for the first 26 hits and the hit window shrinks. Every 10 hits the sky moves on, from Sunset through Dusk, Night and Aurora to Dawn. After 10 hits Pip sometimes reverses, and after 20 some stars drift along the ring.
  - q: Why did my tap count as early or late?
    a: Taps are judged at the instant you press, to the millisecond. One early or late tap ends the run, and the screen tells you by how many milliseconds you missed so you can adjust.

Pip the neon spark zips around a glowing ring under a synthwave sky that drifts from sunset to dawn as you score. Tap as Pip crosses the star; hit its gold centre for a Perfect, and one miss ends the run.
