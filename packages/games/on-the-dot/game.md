faq:
  - q: What is a Perfect?
    a: Pip's centre lands on the gold centre of the star. Perfects in a row are worth more, up to 5 points each; a Good is worth 1.
  - q: What changes as I go?
    a: Pip speeds up for the first 26 hits. Every 10 hits the sky moves on, from sunset through dusk, night and aurora to dawn. After 10 hits Pip sometimes reverses, and after 20 some stars drift along the ring.
  - q: Why did my tap count as early or late?
    a: Taps are judged at the instant you press, to the millisecond. A Good tells you how far off you were, so you can adjust.

Pip the neon spark zips around a glowing ring under a synthwave sky that drifts from sunset to dawn as you score. Tap as Pip crosses the star; hit its gold centre for a Perfect, and one miss ends the run.
