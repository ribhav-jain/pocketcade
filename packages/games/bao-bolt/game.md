faq:
  - q: What happens if I bump into something?
    a: Bao stumbles, slows down for a moment and is safe for about a second and a half. Gulp the gull swoops in and stays for 8 seconds. Bump into anything while Gulp is near and the run ends. Every coin you grab sends Gulp off a little sooner.
  - q: Which obstacle needs which move?
    a: Hop steamer stacks and scuttling crabs, and slide under hanging shop signs. A longer press makes a higher jump, which you need for the tall steamer stacks. Pressing the slide key in mid-air drops you to the ground and straight into a slide.
  - q: How is the score counted?
    a: You score a point for every metre, times your multiplier. Every 10 coins in a row raise the multiplier by one, up to times 5, and a stumble sets it back to 1. Missing a coin does not break the row.
  - q: Does the run ever get easier?
    a: Only in the quiet coin-only stretches, one of which opens each new district. Bao runs faster for the first three minutes, from 6 to 14 world units a second, and the obstacle patterns get harder in each district up to 1,500 metres. After that the four districts repeat in shorter stretches.

Bao, a plucky steamed bun in a red bandana, bolts across the rooftops of a night market. Hop steamer stacks and runaway crabs, slide under hanging shop signs and grab sesame coins while Gulp, a greedy gull in a napkin bib, waits for a second mistake. The route runs through Dumpling Alley, Neon Row, Crane Harbour and Temple Gardens.
