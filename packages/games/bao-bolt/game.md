Bao, a plucky steamed bun in a red bandana, bolts across the lantern-lit rooftops of a night market. Hop steamer stacks and runaway crabs, slide under hanging shop signs and grab sesame coins while Gulp, a greedy gull in a napkin bib, gives chase.
