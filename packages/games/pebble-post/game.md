    keyboard: Down arrow or S
    pointer: Drag down
    touch: Swipe down

    keyboard: Escape or P

faq:
  - q: Which obstacles need which move?
    a: Low ones (snow drifts and ice blocks) you hop over. High ones (icicle arches and banners of frozen mail) you tuck under. Tall ones (ice pillars and parked mail sleds) block the lane, so slide around them. Any of them can also be dodged by changing lane.
  - q: Is there always a way through?
    a: Yes. Every row of obstacles leaves a way through, spaced so there is time for the moves it needs at your speed, from whichever lane you got past the row before in.
  - q: What do the power-ups do?
    a: The scarf takes one hit for you (or wears off after 20 seconds). The mail bell pulls in every letter nearby, from all lanes, for 8 seconds. You can only hold one at a time.
  - q: How is the score counted?
    a: One point for every metre Pebble slides, plus 5 for every letter she collects. She speeds up over the first three minutes or so.

Pebble, a little penguin mail courier in a red beanie, belly-slides down a winding ice canyon with her satchel. Switch lanes, hop the snow drifts, tuck under icicle arches and scoop up every letter, from the snowy canyon to the aurora night and the crystal cave.
