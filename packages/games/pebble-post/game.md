faq:
  - q: Which obstacles need which move?
    a: Low ones (snow drifts and fallen logs) you hop over. High ones (icicle arches and mail banners) you tuck under. Tall ones (ice pillars and parked mail sleds) fill the lane, so change lane. You can also dodge any of them by sliding to another lane.
  - q: Is there always a way through?
    a: Yes. Every row of obstacles leaves at least one lane you can get through, and the rows are spaced so you have time for the moves you need at your current speed.
  - q: What do the power-ups do?
    a: The scarf takes one hit for you, then Pebble blinks safely for a second. It wears off after 20 seconds. The mail bell pulls in letters from every lane, up high and down low, for 8 seconds. You hold one power-up at a time.
  - q: How is the score counted?
    a: You get 1 point for each metre, 5 for each letter and 10 for each obstacle you hop over, tuck under or leave at the last moment. Pebble starts at 18 metres a second and speeds up to 30 over about 2 minutes 15 seconds.

Pebble, a little penguin mail courier, belly-slides down a winding ice canyon with her satchel. Change lanes, hop the drifts, tuck under the icicle arches and scoop up every letter as the scenery moves from snowy canyon to aurora night and a glittering crystal cave.
