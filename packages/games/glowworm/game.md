faq:
  - q: What is the difference between the caves?
    a: In Open cave the edges wrap around, so Wick slips out one side and back in the other. In Crystal cave the edges are walls and a few crystal clusters block the way. Each cave has its own best score.
  - q: What does the golden berry do?
    a: After every eighth berry a golden star berry appears for about six seconds, with a ring that shows the time left. It is worth 50 points instead of 10.
  - q: Does Wick speed up?
    a: Yes, a little with every berry, until Wick reaches top speed.

Guide Wick, a little glowworm, through a dark crystal cave. Gobble glowing berries to grow longer and light up the cave, chase the golden ones before they fade, and whatever you do, don't bite your own tail.
