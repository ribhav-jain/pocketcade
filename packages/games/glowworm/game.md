faq:
  - q: How do I get to the next cave?
    a: Eat 8 glowberries. The cave lights up for a bonus of 50 points and Wick starts again at the left of a new cave, with a new layout and colours, at full length. Wick keeps getting longer and faster across caves.
  - q: What are the glowing tunnels?
    a: In some caves the edges of a row or column wrap around, so Wick leaves one side and comes back on the other. Other caves are walled, and crystals block the way. The cave name and its edge rule show on the screen.
  - q: What does the golden berry do?
    a: Once in each cave, after the fourth berry, a golden star berry appears for 6 seconds, with a ring that shows the time left. It is worth 50 points instead of 10 and does not count toward the 8.
  - q: How do I score more?
    a: A berry is worth 10 points. Eat the next one within 24 moves and each berry in the row is worth 5 more, up to 30. Wick speeds up with every berry from 150 down to 90 milliseconds a move. Fill the whole cave and you win.
coverAlt: Wick, a smiling mint-green glowworm with glowing antennae, curls toward a pink glowberry in a dark cave lit by violet crystals, with a golden star berry and its timer ring above.
---

Guide Wick, a little glowworm, through a series of dark crystal caves. Gobble glowing berries to grow longer, chase the golden ones before they fade and light up the whole cave to move on. Whatever you do, don't bite your own tail.
