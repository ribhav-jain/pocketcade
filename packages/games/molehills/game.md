controls:
  - action: Dig a patch
    keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space or Enter to dig
    pointer: Left-click
    touch: Tap
  - action: Plant or remove a daisy
    keyboard: F or X (M switches between Dig and Plant)
    pointer: Right-click, hold, or switch to Plant
    touch: Hold, or switch to Plant
  - action: Dig around a finished number
    keyboard: Space on the number
    pointer: Click the number
    touch: Tap the number
  - action: Hint
    keyboard: H
    pointer: Hint button
    touch: Hint button
  - action: Pick a size or start a new board
    keyboard: 1, 2 or 3 for Garden, Field or Meadow; N for a new board
    pointer: Size tabs and New board button
    touch: Size tabs and New board button
  - action: Zoom and pan a large board
    keyboard: O to zoom, arrow keys to move the cursor
    pointer: Zoom button, drag to pan
    touch: Zoom button, drag to pan
faq:
  - q: Will I ever have to guess?
    a: No. Every board can be cleared by reasoning from the numbers, and your first dig is always safe. The Hint button points out the next safe patch, or a daisy that may be on a safe patch.
  - q: What do the numbers mean?
    a: A number counts the moles napping in the 8 patches around it. Dig every patch without a mole to clear the garden. Digging a mole's patch wakes it and ends the board.
  - q: What are the board sizes?
    a: Garden is 8 by 10 with 12 moles. Field is 12 by 16 with 32 moles, and Meadow is 16 by 24 with 70. The bigger boards sometimes need you to compare two numbers at once. Each size keeps its own best time and snooze streak.
  - q: Is my board saved?
    a: Yes, on this device, so you can leave a board and come back to it with your time kept.
coverAlt: A sleepy mole in a striped nightcap peeks from its molehill among daisies and numbered soil patches under a sunny sky.
---

Sleepy moles in nightcaps snooze under a sunny garden bed, and each number in the soil counts the moles in the eight patches around it. Dig every safe patch, plant a daisy wherever a mole naps, and clear the bed without a single grumpy wake-up.
