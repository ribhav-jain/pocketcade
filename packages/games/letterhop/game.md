faq:
  - q: What counts as a word?
    a: Any 4-letter word in Letterhop's word list. Each hop must change exactly one letter and cannot repeat a word already on your ladder. Wrong tries are free, and they do not count as hops.
  - q: How is my ladder rated?
    a: By the hops you used against par, which is the fewest hops possible. At par or better you earn three leaves, up to 2 over par earns two, up to 5 over earns one. You have 50 hops before you run out of rungs.
  - q: When does the daily ladder change?
    a: At midnight in your time zone. Everyone gets the same ladder each day, and solving it on consecutive days builds your streak. You can also practise with short, medium or long ladders at any time.
  - q: What if I get stuck?
    a: Climb down a pad to try another word, though the hops stay counted. Show a way reveals one shortest ladder, which ends your attempt and resets the daily streak.

Hop the frog crosses a moonlit pond one lily pad at a time: change a single letter and he leaps to the next pad with a splash. Reach the golden goal word in as few hops as you can, and keep your daily streak flame burning.
