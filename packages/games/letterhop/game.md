  - action: Enter the next word
    keyboard: Type 4 letters in the Next word field

  - action: Hop
    keyboard: Enter
    pointer: Click Hop
    touch: Tap Hop
  - action: Climb down
    keyboard: Ctrl+Z or Cmd+Z with the field empty
    pointer: Click Climb down
    touch: Tap Climb down
  - action: Pause menu
    keyboard: Esc
    pointer: Esc
    touch: Pause button
faq:
  - q: What counts as a word?
    a: Any 4-letter word in Letterhop's word list. Invalid tries are free.
  - q: When does the daily ladder change?
    a: At midnight in your time zone.

Hop the frog crosses a moonlit pond one lily pad at a time: change a single letter and he leaps to the next pad with a splash. Reach the golden goal word in as few hops as you can, and keep your daily streak flame burning.
