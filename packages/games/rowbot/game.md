faq:
  - q: How does Rowdy get sharper?
    a: Each round you win in a row powers Rowdy up, over four steps. Easy blocks your threes more often, Medium and Hard look further ahead, and all three slip less. Lose or forfeit once and the streak ends, and Rowdy resets.
  - q: What are Easy, Medium and Hard?
    a: Easy only looks a move ahead. Medium searches a few moves deep and sometimes plays its second-best move. Hard searches much deeper but not the whole game, so a long plan can still beat it. Each level keeps its own record and best streak.
  - q: What happens in a draw?
    a: A full rack with no four is a draw. It does not end your streak and does not add to it. Who goes first alternates every round.
  - q: Can I stop and keep my streak?
    a: Choose Bank streak on the round card to record your streak and start fresh. The streak you end by losing, forfeiting or banking goes on the Best win streak board. A round in progress is saved if you leave.

Four in a row against Rowdy, a retro robot with an LED face who gloats, gasps and gets dizzy as the coins drop. Keep a win streak going on Easy, Medium or Hard while Rowdy powers up after every round you win.
