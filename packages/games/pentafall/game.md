    keyboard: Up arrow, W or X (turn right); Z or Q (turn left)
    touch: Tap (turn right), or the Turn left button
  - action: Drop faster
    keyboard: Down arrow or S
    touch: Drag down
  - action: Drop now
    keyboard: Space
    touch: Flick down, or the Drop button
  - action: Stash a piece
    keyboard: C or Shift
    touch: Swipe up, or the Stash button
  - action: Pause
    keyboard: Esc or P
    touch: Pause button
faq:
  - q: What is a Full Five?
    a: Clearing five rows with one piece. Stand a straight five-block bar upright and slide it into a five-deep shaft in the pile. Clearing several rows at once scores much more than clearing them one by one.
  - q: How does the stash work?
    a: Stash swaps the falling piece with the one in the stash slot, or with the next piece if the slot is empty. You can stash once per piece, and not if the incoming piece has no room.
  - q: How do levels work?
    a: Every 10 lines you clear takes you up a level, to level 20, and the jellies fall faster. New shapes join the mix at levels 4, 7 and 10. The run ends when a piece settles above the dashed crest.
  - q: Is my run saved?
    a: Yes, on this device, each time a piece settles. Clearing site data removes it.

Eighteen glossy five-block jelly shapes tumble into a neon well twelve cells wide. Fill every cell of a row to clear it, stash a piece for later and slide the long bar into a deep shaft to clear five rows at once.
