faq:
  - q: How do I aim?
    a: Where the comet meets the Skiff's shield sets its angle. A hit near a wing tip sends it off sideways, mid-ship sends it straight up.
  - q: What do the pods do?
    a: Wingspan stretches the Skiff's wings, Scatter splits your comet in three and Stasis slows every comet for a while.
  - q: What happens when I run out of ships?
    a: Clearing every fifth wall repairs a lost ship. If you run out, you can start over or continue from the first wall of the zone you reached, with a fresh score.

Pilot the Skiff, a plucky hover-ship, and bounce a blazing comet through 20 crystal walls adrift in a neon nebula. Plasma mines chain-react, alloy plates make you aim, and glowing pods bring Wingspan, Scatter and Stasis.
