faq:
  - q: How do I aim?
    a: Where the comet meets the Skiff sets its angle. A hit near a wing tip sends it off sideways at about 60 degrees, and mid-ship sends it straight up. Moving the Skiff as you hit adds a little spin.
  - q: What do the pods do?
    a: Break a glowing pod crystal and the pod drops. Catch it with the Skiff. Wingspan stretches the Skiff by half for 15 seconds, Scatter splits your comet into three, and Stasis slows every comet for 10 seconds. Each pod is worth points too.
  - q: What are alloy plates and plasma mines?
    a: Alloy plates never break, so bank the comet off them and aim past them. A plasma mine blows up every mine touching it in a quick chain. Ember, frost and prism crystals take one, two or three hits.
  - q: What happens when I run out of ships?
    a: You start with three ships. Clearing the last wall of each zone of five walls repairs a lost ship. If you run out, you can start over or continue from the first wall of the zone you reached, with a fresh score.

Pilot the Skiff, a plucky hover-ship, and bounce a blazing comet through 20 crystal walls adrift in a neon nebula. Plasma mines chain-react, alloy plates make you aim, and glowing pods bring Wingspan, Scatter and Stasis. Keep the comet in play to build a multiplier.
