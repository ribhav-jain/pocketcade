faq:
  - q: When does the ceiling sink?
    a: After every 6 shots that pop nothing, the whole reef ceiling moves down one row and a new row of bubbles appears at the top. The dots next to Puff show how many shots are left. Shots that pop something don't count.
  - q: How do I score more?
    a: Each popped bubble is worth 10 points, and each bubble that drops because nothing holds it up any more is worth 20. Clearing the whole board gives a 1,000 point bonus and starts the next level.
  - q: What changes from level to level?
    a: Each new level starts with one more row of bubbles, up to 9 rows, and adds bubble kinds, up to 6. Every bubble kind has its own shape as well as its own colour.

Puff, a little pufferfish at the bottom of a coral reef, puffs bubbles up at the bubble ceiling. Bank shots off the walls, match three or more of a kind to pop them, and send whole clusters tumbling before the ceiling sinks down to the reef.
