faq:
  - q: When does the ceiling sink?
    a: After 6 shots in a row that pop nothing, a new row of bubbles pushes in at the top. From the Coral Garden zone on it happens after 5. The dots beside Puff count the shots. A shot that pops something resets the count.
  - q: How do I earn stars?
    a: Clear the level in few shots. Three stars need a shot count within the level's par, shown on the level card. Two stars allow about 40 percent more shots, and one star is for any clear. Each star adds 500 points.
  - q: How is the score counted?
    a: A popped bubble scores 10 and a bubble that drops loose scores 20. Popping on shots in a row raises a multiplier up to times 5. Every fourth pop in a row loads a rainbow bubble, and a freed fish adds 100.
  - q: What are stones, urchins and fish?
    a: Stones never pop, so cut away what holds them up. An urchin bursts when a bubble lands or pops next to it and takes two rings of bubbles with it. Some levels ask you to free every fish by popping or dropping the bubbles they ride in.

Puff, a little pufferfish at the bottom of a coral reef, puffs bubbles up at the bubble ceiling. Bank shots off the walls, match three or more of a kind to pop them and cut whole clusters loose. Forty hand-built levels across four reef zones come first, then new boards keep coming.
