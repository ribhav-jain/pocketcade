    keyboard: D, or the cursor on the deck and Space

  - action: Send a card to its best spot
    keyboard: Cursor on the card and Enter
    pointer: Click the card
    touch: Tap the card
  - action: Move cards by hand
    keyboard: Space to pick up, arrows, Space to place
    pointer: Drag
    touch: Drag
  - action: Undo
    keyboard: U or Ctrl+Z
    pointer: Undo button
    touch: Undo button
  - action: Pause
    keyboard: Esc or P
    pointer: Esc
    touch: Pause button
faq:
  - q: Can every claim be won?
    a: No. Some deals can't be won; undo is unlimited, so try another line before starting a new claim.

Klondike patience at a lantern-lit gold-rush camp at dusk, with a prospector, a lantern keeper and a young miner on the court cards. Bank all four suits from Ace to King in the glowing vaults, with Draw 1 or Draw 3 and unlimited undo.
