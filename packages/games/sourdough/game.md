faq:
  - q: Can every claim be won?
    a: No. The cards are shuffled at random, and some deals cannot be won. Undo is unlimited, so try another line before starting a new claim.
  - q: What is the difference between Draw 1 and Draw 3?
    a: Draw 1 turns over one card from the deck at a time and is the gentler choice. Draw 3 turns over three, and you can play only the top one. Both play through the deck as many times as you like, and each mode keeps its own best time, best moves and win streak.
  - q: What does the Hint button do?
    a: It points at a card you can move, or at the deck when drawing will reach a playable card. If nothing useful is left it tells you to try Undo or a new claim.
  - q: Is my game saved?
    a: Yes, on this device. Your claim is where you left it when you come back. Once every card is face up, the game banks the rest for you.

Klondike patience at a lantern-lit gold-rush camp, with a prospector, a lantern keeper and a young miner on the court cards. Build the seven columns down in alternating colours and bank all four suits from Ace to King in the vaults, with Draw 1 or Draw 3 and unlimited undo.
