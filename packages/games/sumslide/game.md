faq:
  - q: How does merging work?
    a: Every slide moves all the tiles as far as they go. Two equal tiles that meet join into one tile of double the value, and you score the new value. After each move one new 5 or 10 tile appears.
  - q: What is the rainbow Echo tile?
    a: The Echo merges with any number tile and doubles it. A new one arrives after 40 moves, and the Echo meter counts them down. The count waits while an Echo is on the tray.
  - q: What are goals?
    a: The goal is the next tile to build. It starts at 80 and doubles each time. Making it scores its value again as a bonus, gives you an extra Undo and changes the backdrop.
  - q: How many undos do I get?
    a: One to start with, plus one for every goal you reach. Undo takes back your last move only. The game ends when the tray is full and nothing can merge.

Slide every tile on a 4 by 4 tray and merge equal numbers into bigger ones, from 5 up through gold tiles in the thousands. Each goal tile you make pays a bonus, earns an extra Undo and moves the game to a new backdrop, while the rainbow Echo doubles whatever it touches.
