faq:
  - q: Is my progress saved?
    a: Yes, on this device, after every move. Clearing site data removes it.
  - q: What is the rainbow ring tile?
    a: The Echo. It merges with any number and doubles it. A new one arrives every 40 moves, and the Echo meter counts them down; the count pauses while an Echo is on the tray.
  - q: What are goals?
    a: The goal is the next tile to make, starting at 80 and doubling each time. Reaching it scores its value again as a bonus, gives you an extra Undo and changes the backdrop.
  - q: What does the bar under the goal show?
    a: How much of the goal tile's value is already on the tray. Merging never changes the total, so when the bar is full you have everything you need and only have to bring it together.
  - q: How many undos do I get?
    a: One to start with, plus one for every goal you reach. Undo takes back your last move only.

Slide every tile on a 4×4 tray and merge equal numbers into their sum, from 5 up through gold tiles in the thousands. Each goal tile you make pays a bonus, earns an extra Undo and moves the game to a new backdrop, while the rainbow Echo doubles whatever it touches.
