controls:
  - action: Pick a mode and size
    keyboard: Arrow keys to move over the choices, Space or Enter to start
    pointer: Click a Pairs size or Follow
    touch: Tap a Pairs size or Follow
  - action: Open a jar (Pairs)
    keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space or Enter to turn
    pointer: Click a jar
    touch: Tap a jar
  - action: Press a lantern (Follow)
    keyboard: 1 to 4
    pointer: Click a lantern
    touch: Tap a lantern
  - action: Back to the modes (press twice to end a run)
    keyboard: M
    pointer: Modes button
    touch: Modes button
  - action: Pause
    keyboard: Esc
    pointer: Pause button
    touch: Pause button
faq:
  - q: What is Pairs?
    a: Turn over two jars at a time to find matching glyphs, in as few moves as you can. A pair of jars is one move. Mismatched jars stay open for about a second and then close, or you can tap again to close them early. Pick a 4 by 4, 4 by 5 or 6 by 6 grid.
  - q: What is Follow?
    a: Lumi plays a song on four lanterns and you play it back in the same order. Each round adds one note and the song gets faster as it grows. The score is the longest song you repeat correctly.
  - q: What is the second look?
    a: Once per Follow run, a wrong press makes Lumi replay the same song instead of ending the run. The next wrong press ends it.
  - q: Which scores go on the boards?
    a: Fewest moves on the 6 by 6 Pairs grid, and the longest Follow song. Your Pairs boards are saved, so you can leave one and come back to it later.
coverAlt: Lumi, a round purple firefly with a glowing tail and clear wings, hovers over three glass jars glowing with different glyphs in a moonlit meadow.
---

Two memory games in a moonlit meadow, hosted by Lumi the firefly. Peek into glass jars to match every glowing glyph, then play back a lantern song that grows one note each round.
