faq:
  - q: How does a pour work?
    a: The drink on top of the bottle you lift goes into the bottle you choose next, every layer of that flavour that sits on top, as far as there is room. It only pours onto the same flavour or into an empty bottle. A bottle that is full of one flavour gets a cork and is finished.
  - q: How are the stars worked out?
    a: Each level has a par, the number of pours the game's own solver needs. Par or fewer earns 3 stars, up to about a third more earns 2, and anything above that earns 1. Taking a pour back takes it off your count. Hints and the extra bottle are free, and the result notes when you used them.
  - q: What are the layers marked with a question mark?
    a: From level 15, some levels hide a few of the lower layers. A hidden layer shows its flavour when the layer above it has been poured away. Every flavour also has its own symbol, so you never have to tell drinks apart by colour alone.
  - q: Can I get stuck, and is my progress saved?
    a: Every level can be solved, and everyone gets the same levels. You can still pour yourself into a corner; Undo has no limit, and Hint tells you when there is no way through from where you are. Your level, your pours and your stars are saved on this device as you play.

The drinks at Pekoe's tea house have been stacked in the wrong bottles. Lift a bottle and pour its top flavour onto the same flavour or into an empty bottle, until every bottle holds just one. Levels go on without end and grow from three flavours to twelve, with mystery layers later on, and Undo, Hint and one spare bottle are always free.
